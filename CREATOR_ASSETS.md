## 💡 How to Use These Assets with Ultimate Clipper

1. **Background Audio & Music**:
   - Download whooshes, risers, or music tracks from **Mixkit**, **Uppbeat**, or **Meta-open-sound**.
   - Drop the `.mp3` or `.wav` file into `assets/music/` or select it directly via the **Optional Background Music** uploader in **Tab 2**.
   - The clipper will automatically loop and duck it behind creator speech with broadcast-compliant EBU R128 `loudnorm`.

2. **Emojis & Sound Signs**:
   - The clipper automatically maps gaming dialogue to emoji reactions (`😮‍💨`, `😱`, `😂`, `⚡`, `💀`, `🔥`, `🏎️`, `🚨`).
   - You can download high-res 3D emojis from **microsoft/fluentui-emoji** or **Emoji Island** for custom video overlays or thumbnail covers.
